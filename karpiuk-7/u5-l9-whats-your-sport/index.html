Lesson 9

What's Your Sport?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Two new classmates, Kateryna and Ali, are talking during the break. Kateryna: So, Ali, do you do any sport? Ali: Yes, I play cricket. It's very popular in Pakistan, where my family is from. Kateryna: Cricket? I've never seen it. Is it like baseball? Ali: A bit. There's a bat and a ball, and two teams. But a match can last five days! Kateryna: Five days? That's crazy! I prefer something faster. I do fencing. Ali: Fencing? With swords? Kateryna: Yes, but they aren't sharp. We wear white suits and masks. It's like a chess game, but with your body. You must think very fast. Ali: That sounds cool! How long have you done it? Kateryna: Four years. I train three times a week. Ali: Could I come and watch one day? Kateryna: Sure! Come on Thursday. And maybe you can teach me cricket one day. But not for five days! They both laughed. The bell rang, and they ran to their maths lesson.
