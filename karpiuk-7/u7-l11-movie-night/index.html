Lesson 11

Movie Night

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
It's Friday evening. Four friends are at Nadiia's house, deciding what to watch. Nadiia: So, what shall we watch? Any ideas? Bohdan: What about an action film? There's a new one about a car race. Liza: Oh no, not cars again! We watched one last week. How about a comedy? Nadiia: I've seen all the good comedies on this website. What about something scary? Artur: I'm not watching a horror film! I won't sleep tonight. Bohdan: Ha! Are you scared, Artur? Artur: I'm not scared. I just need my sleep. Liza: OK, OK. What about a fantasy film? There's one about a girl who can talk to dragons. It's got really good reviews. Nadiia: That sounds great. How long is it? Liza: An hour and fifty minutes. Bohdan: Fine. But I'm choosing next time! Nadiia: Deal. I'll make the popcorn. Artur, can you bring the blankets? Artur: Sure. And if there's a scary dragon, I'm hiding under one! They all laughed and pressed "Play".
