Lesson 11

A Day in London

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
London is one of the most visited cities in the world. Here is a plan for a day there. Start at the Tower of London, a castle more than nine hundred years old. You can see the Crown Jewels and the famous ravens. People say that if the ravens leave, the kingdom will fall! Then walk across Tower Bridge, the beautiful bridge with two towers. Sometimes it opens to let big ships pass. Take a red double-decker bus to Trafalgar Square, with Nelson's Column and four huge lions. The National Gallery is right there, and it's free! Walk to Buckingham Palace, the King's residence in London. If you are lucky, you can see the Changing of the Guard, with soldiers in red jackets and tall black hats. In the afternoon, have a picnic in Hyde Park or go boating on the lake. In the evening, take a ride on the London Eye and watch the city lights. Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament are just across the river. London is expensive, but many of its museums are free. And remember: in London, cars drive on the left!
