Lesson 2

Is Sport Only for Winners?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
After the sports news on our school radio, our class had a hot discussion: should school sport be about winning, or about having fun? "Winning is important," said Bohdan, who plays in the school football team. "If you don't want to win, why do you play? Competition makes you stronger." "I don't agree," said Nastia. "I'm not very good at sport, and in PE lessons nobody wants me in their team. That's why many students hate PE. Sport should be for everybody." "I think both are important," said Yurii. "In a match you should try to win. But at school the main thing is to move and enjoy it." Our PE teacher listened and then gave an example. "Last year the slowest runner in the school ran in the charity race. She finished last, but everybody cheered for her. She was the real hero of the day." Then she made a decision. Next month our school will have a new "Fun Sports Day": no medals, no points, just funny team games like sack races and three-legged races. "And the teachers will run too!" she added. Everybody laughed.
