Lesson 10

Interview With a Traveller

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Olena Hrytsenko has visited sixty countries. Our school magazine interviewed her when she came to talk to our school. Reporter: When did you start travelling? Olena: When I was a student. I had very little money, so I travelled by train and slept in cheap hostels. Reporter: What was your most unusual trip? Olena: I spent a week with a family of reindeer herders in Norway. We lived in a tent, and it was minus thirty degrees! Reporter: Which country surprised you most? Olena: Japan. Everything is so polite and organised. Even the trains say "sorry" when they are one minute late. Reporter: Have you ever had problems on a trip? Olena: Of course. I once lost my passport in Peru. It took me two weeks to get a new one. But I made friends with the people at the embassy! Reporter: What is your advice for young travellers? Olena: Learn languages, be curious and respect local traditions. And always keep a copy of your passport. Reporter: Where are you going next? Olena: To Mongolia. I want to ride horses across the steppe.
