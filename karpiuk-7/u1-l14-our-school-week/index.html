Our School Week

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Hello! My name is Kateryna, and I'm the class monitor of 7-B. Our teacher asked me to describe a usual week in our class for our school website. On Mondays we start with English, and then we have a double maths lesson. Everybody is sleepy on Monday mornings, so our maths teacher always begins with a funny puzzle. On Tuesdays and Thursdays many of us stay after school for clubs. The robotics team, the choir and the football team all meet on these days. Wednesday is our "eco-day". Every Wednesday our class collects paper and plastic for recycling. Last year we collected four hundred kilograms of paper! Fridays are short. After lessons we often go to the park together or have a pizza at the café near the school. This week is different, though. We are preparing for the school anniversary concert. Right now the boys are learning a dance, and the girls are making decorations. Our class teacher is writing a short play, and I am helping her with the costumes. It's busy, but it's fun. I think our class is the best one in the school!
