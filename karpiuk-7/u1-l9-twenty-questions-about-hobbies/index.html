Lesson 9

Twenty Questions About Hobbies

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our English class played a game called "Twenty Questions". One student thinks of a hobby, and the others ask questions to guess it. Liza went first. She thought of a hobby. "What do you need for it?" asked Ivan. "You need paper and a pencil," said Liza. "Where do you usually do it?" asked Olena. "At home or outside, anywhere," answered Liza. "When did you start?" asked Pavlo. "When I was eight." "Why do you like it?" asked Mark. "Because it helps me to relax and to remember things." "How often do you do it?" asked Dasha. "Almost every day." "Who taught you?" asked Ivan. "My grandmother. She is an artist." "Is it drawing?" asked Olena. "Yes!" said Liza. Then it was Pavlo's turn. His hobby was harder: bird watching. It took fifteen questions! "Which birds do you see most often?" "What do you take with you?" "How long do you wait?" Finally somebody guessed. At the end the teacher said, "Look, you used every question word: what, where, when, why, how, who and which!"
