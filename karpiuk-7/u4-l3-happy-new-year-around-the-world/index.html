Happy New Year Around the World

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
At midnight on the 31st of December, people all over the world welcome the New Year. But the traditions are very different. In Spain people eat twelve grapes at midnight, one grape for each stroke of the clock. Each grape represents a lucky month. It's harder than it sounds! In Denmark people smash old plates on the doors of their friends' houses. According to tradition, the more broken plates you find in the morning, the more friends you have. In Colombia some people walk around the block with an empty suitcase. They hope that the new year will bring lots of travel. In Scotland the first person who enters your house after midnight is very important. A tall, dark-haired man with a piece of coal brings good luck. In Japan bells in Buddhist temples ring 108 times to clean away the bad things of the old year. In Ukraine families decorate a New Year tree, cook a big dinner and watch fireworks. At midnight everybody makes a wish.
