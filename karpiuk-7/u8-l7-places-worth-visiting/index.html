Lesson 7

Places Worth Visiting

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Ukraine has many places that are worth visiting. Our class made a list of our favourites. Lviv: The old city centre is like an open-air museum. You can drink hot chocolate in a café, visit the Opera House and climb the town hall tower. At Christmas the whole city smells of gingerbread. Kamianets-Podilskyi: Here there's a huge stone fortress on a rock above a river canyon. In summer there are knight festivals with battles and old music. The Carpathians: This is the place for hiking in summer and skiing in winter. You can climb Hoverla, the highest mountain in Ukraine, 2,061 metres high. Odesa: The city by the sea is famous for its Opera House, the famous Odesa Steps and its humour. People here love jokes! Uman: Sofiyivka Park is one of the most beautiful parks in Europe, with waterfalls, lakes, grottos and statues. It was a present from a man to his wife. Shatsk Lakes: In the north-west there are more than thirty lakes with clean blue water. It's perfect for swimming, fishing and cycling. Which place is on your list?
