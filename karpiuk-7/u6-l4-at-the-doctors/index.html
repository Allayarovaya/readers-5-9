Lesson 4

At the Doctor's

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Ivanka, 13, didn't feel well for three days. She had a sore throat, a headache and a high temperature. Her mum made an appointment with the family doctor. Doctor: Good morning, Ivanka. What seems to be the problem? Ivanka: I've got a sore throat and a headache. And I feel very tired. Doctor: When did it start? Ivanka: On Monday. On Tuesday I had a temperature of 38.5. Doctor: Let me look at your throat. Say "Ah". Yes, it's red. Now I'll listen to your lungs. Breathe in... and out. Good, your lungs are clear. Mum: Is it a serious disease? Doctor: No, it's not serious. It's a throat infection. The symptoms are typical. I'll give you a prescription for some pills. Take one pill three times a day after meals. Drink a lot of warm tea with honey and stay in bed for a few days. Ivanka: Can I go to school? Doctor: Not this week. You need treatment and rest. Come back for a check-up on Friday. And remember: see your doctor regularly, not only when you are ill. On the way home Ivanka smiled. Three days without maths tests!
