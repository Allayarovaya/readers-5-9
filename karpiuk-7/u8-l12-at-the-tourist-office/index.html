Lesson 12

At the Tourist Office

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Dariia and her mum are on holiday in Edinburgh. They go to the tourist office in the morning. Assistant: Good morning! How can I help you? Mum: Hello. We're here for three days. What can we see? Assistant: You must visit Edinburgh Castle. It's on a rock in the centre of the city. Here's a map. Tickets are 20 pounds for adults and 12 for children. Dariia: Are there any guided tours? Assistant: Yes. A tour guide takes groups around the castle every hour. And in the evening there's a ghost tour in the old town. It's very popular with teenagers! Dariia: A ghost tour? Cool! Mum: What about museums? Assistant: The National Museum of Scotland is free. There's also a cathedral on the Royal Mile and a beautiful bridge over the Firth of Forth. You can go there on an excursion by bus. Mum: Do we need to book the excursion? Assistant: Yes, it's better to book a day before. I can do it for you now. Mum: Great. And we're from Ukraine. Do we need a visa for a day trip to the Highlands? Assistant: No, the Highlands are still in Scotland. No visa needed! Everybody laughed.
