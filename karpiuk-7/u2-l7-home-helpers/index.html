Lesson 7

Home Helpers

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Last month our class visited a small museum of household appliances in our town. It was much more interesting than we expected. In the first room there were very old things. We saw a heavy iron which people heated on the stove, and a wooden washing board. "Imagine washing all your family's clothes by hand," said the guide. "It took a whole day!" In the second room there were the first electrical appliances. The first vacuum cleaner looked like a big metal box on wheels. The first washing machine had no programmes, only one button. There was also an old toaster that could toast only one slice of bread at a time. The third room showed modern appliances. There was a microwave oven that can talk, a robot vacuum cleaner and a hairdryer that is as quiet as a whisper. At the end the guide showed us her favourite thing, an old electric saw. "Every new appliance was an improvement," she said. "Each one gave people more free time." On the way home Maksym said, "I think the dishwasher is the best invention. I hate washing up!"
