Health Myths

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
There are many things people say about health. Some are true, and some are myths. Let's check! "If you go out with wet hair, you catch a cold." Myth! Colds are caused by viruses, not by cold air. But you can feel uncomfortable and cold. "Carrots help you to see better." Partly true. Carrots have vitamin A, which is good for your eyes. But they won't help you see in the dark. "You should drink eight glasses of water a day." It depends. You get water from food and other drinks too. Drink when you are thirsty, and more when you do sport. "Reading in bad light damages your eyes." Myth! It makes your eyes tired, but it doesn't damage them. "An apple a day keeps the doctor away." Not exactly, but fruit is really good for you. Doctors say we should eat five portions of fruit and vegetables a day. "If you swallow chewing gum, it stays in your stomach for seven years." Myth! It goes out of your body in a few days, just like food. So next time your granny tells you something about health, smile and check it!
