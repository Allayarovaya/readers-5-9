Lesson 12

My Food Diary

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our biology teacher asked us to keep a food diary for one week. It's Thursday evening, and this is what I have written so far. This week I have eaten a lot of fruit. I have had an apple every day, and yesterday I tried a mango for the first time. It was sweet and juicy! I have drunk more water than usual. Our teacher says we need about six glasses a day. I haven't reached six yet, but I've drunk four or five every day. I have already eaten fish twice: salmon on Monday and fish soup today. I have never liked fish much, but Mum's fish soup is changing my mind. Unfortunately, I have also eaten too many sweets. My friend has just had a birthday, and there was a huge chocolate cake. I've had three pieces this week! I haven't eaten any fast food yet, and I'm proud of that. Usually I have burgers twice a week. What have I learned? Healthy food can be tasty. And keeping a diary makes you think before you eat that fourth piece of cake.
