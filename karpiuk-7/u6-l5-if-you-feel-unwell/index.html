Lesson 5

If You Feel Unwell

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our school nurse made a poster for the medical room. It has simple rules that are always true. If you cut your finger, you clean it and put on a plaster. If you have a nosebleed, you sit down and lean forward, not backwards. If you touch something very hot, you put your hand under cold water for ten minutes. If you get stung by a bee, you remove the sting and put something cold on it. If you don't drink enough water, you get a headache. If you sit at the computer for hours, your eyes get tired. If you eat too many sweets, your teeth go bad. If you feel dizzy, you sit down and tell an adult. At the bottom of the poster the nurse wrote: "If you don't know what to do, you come to the medical room!"
