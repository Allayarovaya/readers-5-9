Lesson 9

Notes on the Fridge

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
In the Shevchuk family everybody is busy, so they talk through notes on the fridge. Here are the notes from last Monday. Andrii, please take out the garbage before school. And don't forget your PE kit! Love, Mum. Mum, I'm at football practice until six. I took my PE kit, don't worry. There's no bread, by the way. Andrii. Kids, I'm working late today. There is soup in the fridge. Warm it up in the microwave, don't use the oven. Dad. Dad, the soup was delicious! I washed the pot and fed the cat. Can I go to Olia's house on Saturday? Her mum says it's OK. Ira. Ira, yes, you can go, but first tidy your room. It looks like a storm was there! Mum. Everybody, Grandma is coming on Wednesday! Let's clean the flat on Tuesday evening, OK? And somebody buy bread!!! Dad. I bought bread. Two loaves. Andrii. Mum keeps the funniest notes in a box. She says that one day they will be the family's history.
