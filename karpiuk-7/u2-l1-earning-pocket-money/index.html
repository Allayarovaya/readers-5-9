Lesson 1

Earning Pocket Money

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
How do teenagers earn pocket money? We asked four of them. "My parents pay me for extra chores. Washing up is part of my normal duties, so I don't get money for it. But if I hoover the whole flat or iron my dad's shirts, I get fifty hryvnias. I'm saving for new headphones." Liliia, 13 "I deliver leaflets for a pizza place on Saturday mornings. I walk about five kilometres! It's tiring, but I like being outside, and I earn my own money." Vadym, 14 "My parents are divorced, so I live with my mum during the week and with my dad at weekends. I split my chores between two homes. At Mum's I take out the garbage and feed the cat. At Dad's I help him in the garage." Olha, 12 "I don't get pocket money for chores. My parents say that everybody in a family must help for free. But I sell my old toys and books online, and I save everything." Denys, 13 So, should parents pay their children for chores? What do you think?
