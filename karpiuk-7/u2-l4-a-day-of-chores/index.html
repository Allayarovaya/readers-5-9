Lesson 4

A Day of Chores

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Saturday is cleaning day in the Hrytsenko family. Everybody has a job, and nobody can go out until the flat is clean. At nine o'clock Dad opens all the windows. "Fresh air first!" he says. Then he hoovers the carpets in the living room and the hall. Mum cleans the bathroom and the kitchen. She is fast and very careful. She says that a clean kitchen is a healthy kitchen. Bohdana, 12, dusts the shelves and waters the plants. She has a lot of plants in her room, about twenty, so this job takes half an hour. Her brother Lev, 14, changes the sheets on all the beds and takes the old newspapers and bottles to the recycling bins. At twelve o'clock the family meets in the kitchen. They check the list on the fridge. Every job has a tick. Then Dad makes his famous Saturday omelette, and everybody eats together. "I hate cleaning," says Lev, "but I love the feeling when it's finished. The flat looks new, and the rest of the weekend is free!"
