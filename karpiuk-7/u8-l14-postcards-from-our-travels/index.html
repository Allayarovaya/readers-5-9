Lesson 14

Postcards From Our Travels

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
At the end of the school year our English teacher asked us to write imaginary postcards from places we studied. Here are three of them. Dear Mum and Dad, I'm in Scotland! Today I climbed to Edinburgh Castle, and the view was amazing. Tomorrow we're going on a boat trip on Loch Ness. I hope to see the monster! Love, Kira Hi Danylo, Greetings from Tokyo! The city is huge and very clean. I've eaten sushi every day, and I tried to read the signs, but it's impossible. Yesterday I saw a robot that serves coffee! See you soon, Maks Dear Grandma, I'm writing from Kamianets-Podilskyi. The fortress is like a castle from a fairy tale. There was a knight festival, and a real knight let me hold his sword. It was very heavy! I've bought you a little clay jug as a present. Kisses, Vira Our teacher put all the postcards on a big map on the classroom wall. Now the map is full of colourful cards from all over the world. "Next year," she said, "I hope you will send me real postcards!"
