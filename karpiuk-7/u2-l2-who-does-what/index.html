Lesson 2

Who Does What?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
The Moroz family has five people and one big problem: housework. Last month Mum made a chart and put it on the fridge. Dad always cooks dinner on weekdays. He is a great cook, and he likes trying new recipes. He sometimes burns the potatoes, but nobody complains. Mum usually does the shopping and pays the bills. She never irons. "Life is too short for ironing," she says. Grandma lives with them. She always waters the plants and usually makes breakfast. She never washes up, because the family has a rule: whoever cooks doesn't wash up. Ivan, 13, takes out the garbage every evening and sometimes hoovers his room. He never makes his bed in the morning. "Why make it if I sleep in it again in the evening?" he asks. His sister Nina, 10, usually feeds the cat and always sets the table. She sometimes forgets to clean her teeth, but that's not housework! The chart works quite well. There are fewer arguments now, and everybody knows their duties.
