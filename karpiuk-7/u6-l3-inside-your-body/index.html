Lesson 3

Inside Your Body

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Your body is an amazing machine. Every second, many organs work together to keep you alive. Your heart is a strong muscle about the size of your fist. It beats about 100,000 times a day and pumps blood all around your body. When you run, it beats faster. Your lungs are like two big sponges in your chest. When you breathe in, they fill with air. Oxygen from the air goes into your blood. When you breathe out, they push out carbon dioxide. Smoking damages the lungs, so they can't work well. Your tongue is not only for talking. It helps you to taste food: sweet, salty, sour and bitter. A doctor often asks you to show your tongue, because its colour can show if you are ill. Your brain is the boss of the whole body. It uses about one fifth of all your energy, even when you sleep. How can you help your body? Move every day, eat fruit and vegetables, drink water and sleep for eight or nine hours. Your heart and lungs will thank you!
