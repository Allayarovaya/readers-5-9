Lesson 7

An Interview With a Young Chef

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Ivanna Moroz is only fifteen, but she has already won two cooking competitions and has her own cooking channel. Our school reporter asked her some questions. How long have you cooked? Since I was seven. My grandma taught me to make varenyky, and I've loved cooking ever since. What is the most difficult dish you have made? A French cake with six layers. It took me two days, and I've made it only once! How many videos have you made? About a hundred and twenty. My little brother films them on his phone. Which countries' food have you tried to cook? Italian, Georgian, Mexican and Thai. Thai food is my favourite now. Where have you travelled with your cooking? I've cooked at food festivals in Lviv and Kyiv. Last year I went to a competition in Poland. Why have you chosen cooking? Because food brings people together. When I cook for my family, everybody sits at one table and talks. What have you learned from your mistakes? That burned pancakes are not the end of the world!
