Lesson 7

Child Stars

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Some actors become famous when they are still at school. Is it a dream or a difficult life? Ruslana, 14, from Dnipro, has played in three films and one TV series. She started acting when she was eight, after a casting at her school. "It's fun, but it's real work," she says. "Sometimes we film for ten hours a day. I do my homework between scenes." Ruslana has a tutor on set who helps her with school subjects. Her mum is always with her. "I miss my friends sometimes," she admits. "When they go to a birthday party, I'm often in another city filming." Many child stars have problems when they grow up. People only remember them as children, and it's hard to find new roles. Some stop acting and choose other jobs. But others become great actors. Some famous Hollywood stars started their careers as children and won important prizes as adults. Ruslana knows what she wants. "I want to study at a theatre university and play in a real theatre. Films are great, but nothing is better than a live audience."
