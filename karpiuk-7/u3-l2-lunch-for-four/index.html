Lesson 2

Lunch for Four

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
It's Saturday. Mum and Dad are at work, and Marta, 13, has promised to cook lunch for herself, her brother Bohdan and two friends who are coming at one o'clock. Marta: Bohdan, what do we have in the fridge? Bohdan: Let me see. Eggs, cheese, tomatoes, some chicken and a cucumber. And there's rice in the cupboard. Marta: Great! We can make chicken with rice and a salad. Bohdan: Can I help? I can wash the vegetables. Marta: Yes, please. Wash the tomatoes and the cucumber, and then cut them. Be careful with the knife! Bohdan: What are you doing with the chicken? Marta: I'm cutting it into small pieces. Then I'll fry it with onion. Can you put the rice in the pot? Two cups of rice and four cups of water. Bohdan: Done! What about dessert? Marta: There are apples. Let's bake them with honey and cinnamon. At one o'clock the doorbell rang. The friends came in and smelled the kitchen. "Wow! Did your mum cook this?" asked Dmytro. "No," said Bohdan proudly. "We did!"
