Lesson 3

About My School

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My name is Alec. I go to school. My school is near my house. There are three floors in the school. On the ground floor there is a cloakroom, a canteen and a gym. We leave our coats and jackets in the cloakroom. We eat in the canteen after the third lesson. And we have PE classes in the gym. The teachers’ room is on the first floor. The library is on the first floor, too. There is an assembly hall on the second floor. We have concerts there, sing and dance. There are more than 1,000 children in my school. Our classroom is on the ground floor. There is a small stadium behind our school. We run and play games there. There are a lot of trees and beautiful flowers near my school. I like it very much.
