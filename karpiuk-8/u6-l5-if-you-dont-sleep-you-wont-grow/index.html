Lesson 5

If You Don't Sleep, You Won't Grow

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My grandmother has an answer for everything, and every answer starts with "if". I have collected her sayings for a week. Monday. I didn't want to eat breakfast. "If you don't eat in the morning, you won't have energy for the day," she said. "And if you have no energy, you will fall asleep in Maths." Tuesday. I was playing a game at midnight. "If you don't sleep, you won't grow. If you don't grow, you will not reach the top shelf. And that is where I keep the sweets." Wednesday. I went out without a hat. "If you catch a cold, you will miss the school trip on Friday." Thursday. I refused to eat soup. "If you eat only sandwiches, your stomach will hurt. If your stomach hurts, I will take you to the doctor. And you know that he gives injections." Friday. I was sitting with my phone for three hours. "If you sit so much, your back will look like a question mark." Saturday. I helped her in the garden. "If you work in the fresh air, you will sleep like a baby tonight." I did. Sunday. I asked her, "Grandma, is there anything that will not happen?" She thought. "If you are kind to people, you won't be alone. If you laugh every day, you will live long. I am eighty-two, and I have laughed every day, even on bad days." "And if I don't listen to you?" "Then you will learn everything yourself," she said. "But it will take longer."
