How to Write an Article about Your Country

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
An international school magazine asked students from different countries to write a short article: "My Country in 150 Words". Our teacher showed us how to do it, step by step. Step one: a mind map. Write "Ukraine" in the middle of a page. Draw lines to the words that come to your mind: position, nature, climate, people, food, history, symbols. Don't think too long. Then choose only four. You can't put everything into 150 words. Step two: a title. It must catch the reader's eye. "Ukraine" is boring. "A Country of Sunflowers and Brave Hearts" is better. Step three: the first sentence. Start with a question or a surprising fact. "Did you know that the geographical centre of Europe is in Ukraine?" Step four: the body. Write one short paragraph for each idea. Use facts and numbers, but not too many. Add something personal: what you can see from your window or what your grandmother cooks. Step five: linking words. They hold the text together: first of all, also, however, for example, that is why. Step six: the conclusion. Say why you love your country or invite the reader to visit it. Step seven: check. Count the words. Read the article aloud. If you get bored, the reader will get bored too. Here is the beginning of my article: "Have you ever seen a sea made of flowers? Come to Ukraine in July. Sunflower fields stretch to the horizon, yellow under a blue sky, just like our flag..." I wrote 149 words. My teacher said that the missing word was "welcome". So I added it.
