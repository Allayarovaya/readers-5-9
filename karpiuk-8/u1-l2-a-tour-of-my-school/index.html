Lesson 2

A Tour of My School

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Last week a group of students from Poland visited our school, and I was their guide. I had practised for three days. Here is my tour. "Welcome! We are standing in the hall. On your left you can see the lockers. Every student has one. Mine is number 214, and please don't open it. It is not ready for guests. The stairs in front of you lead to the first floor. But first, look to the right. That is the canteen. The school kitchen is behind it. Lunch is at twelve, and the queue starts at five to twelve. Next to the canteen is the assembly hall. It has a real stage and a piano. We have concerts and meetings there. Last year I played a tree in a school play. I had no words. Now let's go upstairs. This is the science lab. We have twelve microscopes and one skeleton. His name is Stepan. Please don't shake his hand. It falls off. The library is at the end of the corridor. It is my favourite place, because it is quiet and the librarian gives us tea in winter. On the second floor there are the Art classroom and the Music classroom. And this door is the staff room. Students mustn't go in. Nobody knows what teachers do there. The head teacher's office is next to it. Finally, the gym is in a separate building. It has rackets, balls and a climbing wall. Any questions?" A Polish boy put up his hand. "Can we see locker 214?"
