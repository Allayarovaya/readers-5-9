Lesson 5

Postcards in Six Tenses

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My sister Bohdana is spending a year in Ireland as an exchange student. She sends me a message every Sunday. Our English teacher says that her messages are a perfect grammar lesson. "Hi! I am sitting in a cafe in Dublin and drinking hot chocolate. It is raining, of course. It rains here almost every day, but people don't carry umbrellas. They just walk faster. I have been here for three months, and I have already visited four cities. I have learned to understand the Irish accent. Well, almost. Yesterday I went to the Cliffs of Moher. While I was standing on the edge, the wind was blowing so hard that I couldn't hear my own voice. I was taking a photo when my hat flew away. It is in the Atlantic Ocean now. Before I came here, I had never seen the ocean. I had only read about it. It is bigger than I had imagined. My host family is great. Mrs O'Brien cooks a huge breakfast every morning. Mr O'Brien tells jokes that I don't understand, but I laugh anyway. Their son is teaching me Irish dancing at the moment. I am terrible at it. Last week my class had a culture day. I had made varenyky the night before, and everybody tried them. They had never eaten anything like that. Now they ask for more. I miss you all. I have bought you a present, but I won't tell you what it is. What are you doing now? Has Mum repaired my bike? Love, Bohdana"
