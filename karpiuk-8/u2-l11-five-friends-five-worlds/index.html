Reader Karpiuk 8 · Unit 2 · Friends Forever
Lesson 11

Five Friends, Five Worlds

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
I have five close friends. People are surprised, because we are so different. If you saw us together, you would think that we had met by mistake. But each of them gives me something special. Polina is the brain. She reads encyclopedias for fun and knows why the sky is blue. When I don't understand Physics, I go to her. She explains everything with apples and spoons. Hlib is the clown. He can make a whole bus laugh. Once he made our strict head teacher laugh so much that she forgot why she had called him to her office. But Hlib is also the first person who notices when somebody is sad. Zlata is the artist. She draws on everything: notebooks, hands, the walls of her room. She sees beautiful things where I see only an old fence. She has taught me to look. Taras is the sportsman. He gets up at six to run. I have joined him three times. I hated every minute, and I felt fantastic afterwards. He never laughs at slow runners. Emma is the quiet one. She moved here from Mariupol two years ago. She doesn't talk much, but when she says something, everybody listens. She is the most loyal person I know. And me? I asked them what I give to the group. Hlib said, "You are the glue. Without you we would be five strange people. With you we are friends." I think that it is the best thing anybody has ever said to me. A friend is not a copy of you. A friend is another world.
