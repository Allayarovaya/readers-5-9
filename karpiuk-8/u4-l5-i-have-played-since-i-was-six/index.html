Lesson 5

I Have Played Since I Was Six

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our school magazine interviewed three students who play music. The question was simple: "How long?" Yeva, 14, a violinist: "I have played the violin since I was six. That is eight years! I have had the same teacher for all this time. She is very strict, and I have cried at her lessons more than once. But I have loved music since my first concert. I have been a member of the city youth orchestra for two years. We have already played in three countries." Demian, 15, a guitarist: "I haven't played for long. I have had my guitar since last Christmas, so for ten months. I have learned from videos. I have known four chords since January, and it is enough for about two hundred songs. My neighbours have complained twice since I got an amplifier." Mariana, 13, a singer: "I have sung since I could speak. My mum says that I sang before I talked. I have been in the school choir for three years, and I have written my own songs since last summer. I have never shown them to anybody. Well, since this interview, everybody knows." And the editor? "I have wanted to play the drums since I was ten," she writes. "I have dreamed about it for four years. I haven't started yet, because we live in a flat on the fifth floor. But I have just found a music school with a room for drummers. Wish me luck. And wish luck to my neighbours."
