Lesson 6

Five Websites I Can't Live Without

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My IT teacher asked us to make a list of the websites that we really use and to explain why. "Not social media," she said. "Useful ones." Here is my list. Number one is an online encyclopedia. I use it for almost every school project. It is fast, and it has articles about everything, from black holes to the history of pizza. But my teacher is right: I always check the facts in one more source. Number two is a website with free online courses. Last winter I did a course in basic programming there. It has short video lessons and tests. Nobody gives you marks, so you learn only because you want to. Number three is a map service. It shows the route, the bus timetable and even how crowded the bus is. Thanks to it, I am late less often. Not never. Less often. Number four is the website of our town library. You can search the catalogue, book a novel and get a message when it is ready. I didn't know that our library was so modern. Number five is a website for learning English. It has songs with missing words, short news for learners and games. I do ten minutes a day. What makes a website good? I think that there are four things. It is easy to use. It loads quickly. It doesn't have too many ads. And you can trust its information. My teacher added a fifth thing: "A good website makes you want to close it and go and do something." I am still thinking about that.
