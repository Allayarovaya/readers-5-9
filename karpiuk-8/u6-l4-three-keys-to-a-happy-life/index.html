Lesson 4

Three Keys to a Happy Life

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Scientists who study health often say the same thing: there is no magic pill. But there are three simple keys. They are free, and you already have them. Key one: sleep. When it gets dark, your brain produces a hormone called melatonin, which makes you sleepy. The light from a phone screen tells your brain that it is still daytime, so less melatonin is produced. Therefore, you can't fall asleep. Teenagers need about nine hours of sleep. During sleep your body grows, and your brain saves what you have learned. A student who sleeps well is more productive than a student who studies all night. Key two: food. Your body needs nutrients: proteins, vitamins, minerals and healthy fats. You get them from vegetables, fruit, fish, eggs, nuts and whole grains. Junk food gives you a lot of energy but few nutrients. It is also hard for your digestive system. People who eat too much of it often become overweight and feel tired. Key three: movement. When you move, your heart gets stronger, and your brain gets more oxygen. Exercise also helps you to overcome stress. After a walk or a game, problems look smaller. You don't have to be an athlete. Thirty minutes a day is enough to feel the difference. The three keys work together. If you move during the day, you sleep better at night. If you sleep well, you don't want so much sugar. If you eat well, you have energy to move. The door to a happy life is not locked. Many people just forget where they have put the keys.
