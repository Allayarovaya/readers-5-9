Lesson 13

Why I Am Learning English

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
On the last day of the school year our teacher gave us our own letters. We had written them in September and forgotten about them. The topic was "Why I am learning English". I opened mine. "Dear me in June, I am writing this on the second of September. To be honest, I am learning English because I have to. It is in the timetable between Maths and PE. I am not sure that I will ever need it. I hardly understand songs, and I have never spoken to a foreigner. I hope that you are better than me." I laughed. Then I took a pen and wrote an answer. "Dear me in September, You were wrong, and I am glad. This year I have read my first book in English. It was thin, and it had pictures, but I read it to the end. I have written to a girl in Canada, who has become my friend. I have watched a film without subtitles and understood more than half. I have learned that English is spoken on every continent. It is the language of the Maori haka and Aboriginal stories, of Scottish mountains and American deserts. It is used by pilots, scientists and programmers. If you know it, you can ask the world a question and understand the answer. And one more thing. Thanks to English, I can tell the world about my country. When my friend from Canada asked me about Ukraine, I found the words. That was the day when I understood why I am learning it. I am not learning English because I have to. I am learning it because I have something to say. See you in the ninth form."
