Lesson 4

Absolutely Brilliant and Totally Boring

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our class teacher asked us to write honestly about our school subjects. "Don't write 'I like everything'," she said. "I won't believe you." Here is my answer. Maths is completely different this year. Last year I hardly understood anything. Now we have a new teacher who explains everything with football examples. I am somewhat surprised, but I actually like it. Ukrainian literature is absolutely brilliant. We read a novel, and then we argue about the characters. Last week the lesson was so interesting that nobody heard the bell. Chemistry is a little frightening. I am a bit afraid of the experiments, because my friend Luka has already burned his sleeve. But the lessons are never boring. Geography is somewhat difficult for me. I can hardly remember the names of all the rivers. I am quite good at maps, though. PE is totally my subject. I am absolutely happy when we play volleyball. I am a little less happy when we run three kilometres. History is quite interesting, but there are too many dates. I completely forget them the day after the test. And English? I am not saying this because my English teacher will read it. It is really useful. I can hardly imagine my future without it. Last summer I spoke to tourists from Norway, and they totally understood me. Is there a subject that I absolutely hate? No. There are only subjects that I haven't understood yet. My teacher wrote at the bottom: "Absolutely honest. A little too long. Totally you."
