Lesson 14

A Postcard from Every Region

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
For our final project in this unit each student had to "send a postcard" from one region of Ukraine. We put them all on a big map. Here are a few. From Zakarpattia: "Greetings from the mountains! This region is mountainous and green. Sheep cheese is made here in the old way. In spring a whole valley is covered with wild daffodils." From Lviv: "Hello from the city of lions! The old centre has been included in the UNESCO list. The streets smell of coffee and chocolate. I have counted forty lions and I am not finished." From Poltava: "Greetings from the heart of Ukraine! The land is flat and the soil is fertile. Halushky, our famous dish, even have a monument here." From Odesa: "Hello from the Black Sea! The city was founded in 1794. It is a big port, and the people are known for their jokes. The stairs to the sea have 192 steps. I have run up all of them." From Kherson: "Greetings from the steppe! The sweetest watermelons are grown here. Not far away there are real sand dunes, like in a desert." From Kharkiv: "Hello from a big industrial and student city. Its main square is one of the largest in Europe. The city is strong, like its people." From Chernihiv: "Greetings from one of the oldest towns in Ukraine. Its churches are almost a thousand years old." When the map was full, our teacher looked at it for a long time. "Different landscapes, different dishes, different jokes," she said. "And one home."
