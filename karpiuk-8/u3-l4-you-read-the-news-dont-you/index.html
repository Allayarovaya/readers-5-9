Reader Karpiuk 8 · Unit 3 · Do We Need The Mass Media?
Lesson 4

You Read the News, Don't You?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My little sister Ivanka is seven, and she has discovered tag questions. Now she uses them all the time. Yesterday she interviewed our grandfather for her "newspaper", which has one page and one reader. "Grandpa, you are old, aren't you?" "I am experienced," said Grandpa. "That is different." "You read the newspaper every day, don't you?" "I do. I have read it for fifty years." "You didn't have the internet when you were a boy, did you?" "No, we didn't. We had one radio for the whole street." "That was boring, wasn't it?" "Not at all. Everybody came to our yard to listen to football matches. It was like a stadium." "You can't use a tablet, can you?" "I can! Your mother has taught me. I can even send photos." "But you have never made a vlog, have you?" "No, I haven't. Have you?" "Not yet. I am going to make one about you. You will help me, won't you?" "Of course I will. What will it be about?" "About old things. You have got an old camera, haven't you?" "I have. It still works. It is in the wardrobe." "We aren't going to tell Mum, are we?" "Why not?" "Because it is a surprise. Journalists don't tell their secrets, do they?" Grandpa laughed. "You will be a great journalist. You ask a lot of questions, and you already know the answers, don't you?" Ivanka thought for a second. "Yes, I do," she said. "But it is polite to ask, isn't it?"
