Lesson 4

The Book That I Was Looking For

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our school library is small, and it has a problem. Students come in and say things like, "I need that book. You know. The blue one." So the librarian, Mr Pavlo, started a game. On the door there is a board with the title "The book that...". Students write descriptions, and other students guess. Here are some from this month. "I am looking for a book that has a wizard school in it and a boy who has a scar." "I need the novel which tells about a girl who lives with her grandfather in the mountains." "Is there a story about a man who travels around the world in eighty days?" "I want the book that our teacher was reading to us in the fifth form. It was about a boy whose nose grew when he lied." "Where is the book which has a map of an island on the first page? There is a pirate who has one leg." Mr Pavlo also writes his own cards. "This is the shelf where you can find poems. This is the box where you should put the books that you have read. And this is the librarian who doesn't like students who fold pages." The game works. Students who had never come to the library now come to guess. Last week somebody wrote, "I am looking for a book that will change my life." Under it there were eleven different answers from eleven different people. Mr Pavlo didn't remove the card. "That is the best thing about books," he said. "The book that changes your life is never the same for two readers."
