Reader Karpiuk 8 · Unit 2 · Friends Forever
Lesson 9

Where R U?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
It is Saturday morning. Four friends are trying to make plans in their group chat. It is harder than it looks. Lida (9.02): Good morning! What are we doing today? Stas (9.40): sleeping Vlad (9.45): I am free after 12. I have to help my dad in the garage first. Nina (9.47): I have a music lesson till 1. How about 2 pm? Lida (9.48): OK, 2 pm. Where? Stas (10.15): pizza? Vlad (10.16): We had pizza last week. Let's go bowling. Nina (10.20): Bowling is expensive. What about the park? The weather is great. Lida (10.21): Park + ice cream. Everybody agree? Vlad and Nina (10.22): OK! Stas (10.50): which park Lida (10.51): The one near the river. At the fountain. 2 pm. Don't be late! At two o'clock Lida, Vlad and Nina are standing at the fountain. Lida (14.10): Stas, where are you? Stas (14.12): at the fountain Lida (14.12): We are at the fountain too. We can't see you. Stas (14.14): big fountain, a lot of pigeons Nina (14.15): There are no pigeons here. Send a photo. Stas sends a photo. It is a fountain. It is in the wrong park, on the other side of the town. Vlad (14.17): That is Central Park. We said "near the river"! Stas (14.18): oh. coming. 20 min. buy me ice cream Lida (14.19): Next time I will call you. With my voice. Like in the old days.
