Lesson 10

The Day Our Class Made the News

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Last spring our class made a real news programme. It was our project for the unit about the mass media, and it took two weeks. First we chose the jobs. Kyrylo and Anna became the presenters, because they have clear voices and don't laugh at the wrong moment. I was a reporter. Bohdan, who has a good camera, was the cameraman. Lesia was the editor. She checked every text and every fact. Then we looked for news. The rule was simple: only true stories from our school and our neighbourhood, and each one from a reliable source. We found five. The school football team had won the city cup. The canteen had a new menu. A seventh-form student had found a wallet and returned it to its owner. The old library was going to get new computers. And a stork had built a nest on the school chimney. I interviewed the cook about the menu. I asked, "The pupils will like the new soup, won't they?" She said, "They had better." The filming was hard. Kyrylo said "Good evening" eleven times before he stopped laughing. The stork flew away exactly when Bohdan turned on the camera. Lesia cut the programme to seven minutes. We posted it on the school website and the school channel. In three days it had nine hundred views, which is more than the number of students in our school. The best comment came from somebody's grandmother: "Finally, some good news." Now we make a programme every month. The stork has become our symbol. It is in every issue.
