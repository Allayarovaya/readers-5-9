Reader Karpiuk 8 · Unit 2 · Friends Forever
Lesson 5

We Understand Each Other

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My grandparents have been married for forty-six years. Last Sunday the whole family came to celebrate, and I watched them carefully. I wanted to understand their secret. They met each other at a village dance in 1978. Grandpa says that they looked at each other for the whole evening and didn't say a word. Grandma says that he stepped on her foot. They are very different. Grandpa is loud, and Grandma is quiet. He loves fishing, and she loves her garden. But they help each other all the time. He carries her heavy buckets. She finds his glasses, which are usually on his head. They don't always agree with each other. They argue about the TV, about the tomatoes and about how to cook borshch. But they never go to bed angry. That is their rule. At the table Grandpa told the same story for the hundredth time. Grandma smiled as if she was hearing it for the first time. All the relatives were talking to one another, laughing and passing dishes to one another. My cousins and I were sending one another funny photos under the table. My aunts were giving one another advice that nobody had asked for. In the evening I asked Grandma, "What is the secret?" She thought for a minute. "We listen to each other. And we forgive each other quickly. Life is too short for long arguments." "And she laughs at my jokes," shouted Grandpa from the garden. "I have heard each of them four hundred times," said Grandma quietly. "But he doesn't have to know that."
