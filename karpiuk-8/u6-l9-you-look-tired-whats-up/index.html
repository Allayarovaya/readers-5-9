Lesson 9

You Look Tired. What's Up?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
It is Monday morning. Vira meets her classmate Luka near the lockers. Vira: Hi, Luka! You look tired. What's up? Luka: Hi. I went to bed at two. I was finishing a game. Vira: At two? On Sunday night? No wonder you look like a zombie. Luka: Thanks. That is very kind. Vira: Sorry. But seriously, do you often go to bed so late? Luka: Almost every night. I can't fall asleep earlier. I lie in bed, and my head is full of thoughts. Vira: I used to have the same problem. Do you look at your phone in bed? Luka: Of course. Everybody does. Vira: That is the reason. The light from the screen wakes your brain up. If you put the phone away an hour before bed, you will fall asleep faster. Luka: An hour? What shall I do for a whole hour? Vira: Read a paper book. Have a warm shower. Listen to quiet music. It worked for me. Luka: Hmm. And what about breakfast? I never have time in the morning. Vira: If you get up ten minutes earlier, you will have time. You should eat something. Even a banana is better than nothing. Luka: You sound like my mum. Vira: Your mum is a clever woman. Why don't you try it for a week? If it doesn't help, I will buy you a pizza. Luka: And if it helps? Vira: Then you will buy me one. Luka: Deal. How are you so full of energy on Monday? Vira: I went to bed at ten. And I had a banana.
