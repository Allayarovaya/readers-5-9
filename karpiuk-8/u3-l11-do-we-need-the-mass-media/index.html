Do We Need the Mass Media?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
At the end of the unit our teacher wrote one question on the board: "Do we need the mass media?" We had ten minutes to write an answer. Here are four of them. Vladyslav: "Yes, we do. Without the media we would know only what happens in our street. Thanks to journalists, I know about a flood in another country, a new law and tomorrow's weather. A person who doesn't know what is happening can't make good decisions." Ilona: "Yes, but we need to be careful. Not everything in the media is true. Some websites publish false stories to get more clicks. I check three things: who wrote it, when, and whether other sources say the same." Maksym: "I think that we need less of it. News is everywhere: on the phone, on TV, on the radio in the bus. Most of it is bad news, and it makes people nervous. I have turned off notifications. I read the news once a day, in the evening, for fifteen minutes." Yuliana: "For me the media are not only news. They are music programmes, podcasts about science, films and interviews with interesting people. My favourite radio programme taught me more about space than my textbook." The teacher read all the answers. "You are all right," she said. "The media are a tool, like a knife. You can cut bread with it, or you can cut your finger. You need it, and you need to know how to hold it." Then she gave us homework: to watch the evening news with our parents and to find one piece of good news. It took me twenty minutes. But I found it.
