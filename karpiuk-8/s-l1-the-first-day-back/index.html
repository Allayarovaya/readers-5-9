Lesson 1

The First Day Back

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
The first of September is a strange day. You are happy and a little sad at the same time. This year I am in the eighth form, and I decided to write down every question that I heard on the first day. At 7.30 Mum asked, "Where is your white shirt?" I didn't know. We found it under my bed. At 8.15 I met my best friend Orest near the school gate. "Where did you spend the summer?" he asked. "Why didn't you answer my messages in August?" I explained that I had been at my grandmother's village, where the internet works only on the hill behind the church. In the corridor everybody was asking the same things. "How was your summer?" "Who is our new Maths teacher?" "When does the first lesson start?" "Which classroom are we in?" Our class teacher, Mrs Hanna, came in with a girl we didn't know. "This is Yustyna. She has moved here from Chernihiv." We asked her, "How old are you?" "What music do you like?" "Whose backpack is that? It is cool!" She smiled and answered every question. Then Mrs Hanna asked us, "What did you read in summer?" Suddenly nobody had questions. Everybody was looking at the floor. At home Dad asked, "How many lessons did you have?" "Five." "What was the best moment?" I thought for a minute. "When Orest said that he had missed me. He didn't use these words, of course. He said, 'Where have you been all summer?' But I understood."
