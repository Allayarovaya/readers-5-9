Lesson 6

Rules That Nobody Wrote

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Every school has rules on the wall. Ours has ten. Don't run in the corridors. Don't be late. Switch off your phone during lessons. Wear indoor shoes. And so on. Everybody knows them, and almost everybody follows them. But there are other rules. Nobody has written them down, and no teacher has ever explained them. You just learn them. I have collected the unwritten rules of our school for new students. One. The table by the window in the canteen belongs to the eleventh form. You can sit there only when you are in the eleventh form. Two. You must never touch Mr Petro's chalk. He has a special piece, and he knows when somebody has used it. Three. If the PE teacher asks, "Who wants to carry the mats?" , you should look at your shoes. Four. You have to say hello to the cleaner, Mrs Liuba. She knows everything about everybody, and she remembers who is polite. Five. You can copy homework in the cloakroom, but you can't do it in the library. The librarian has very good ears. Six. If somebody cries in the toilet, you don't laugh. You ask if they need help or you go away quietly. Seven. On the last day before the holidays you may bring a cake. You must share it with the whole class, including the teacher. The written rules keep the school in order. The unwritten ones make it a home. When my little sister starts here next year, I will give her this list. It is more useful than a map.
