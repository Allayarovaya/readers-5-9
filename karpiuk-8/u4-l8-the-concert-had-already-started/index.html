Lesson 8

The Concert Had Already Started

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My friend Hanna had waited for this concert for half a year. She had bought the ticket in March. She had learned all the songs by heart. She had even chosen her clothes a week before. And then everything went wrong. On the day of the concert she left home at five. The concert was at seven, and the hall was only forty minutes away. But when she got to the bus stop, the bus had just left. The next one came in twenty-five minutes. She was sitting on the bus when she suddenly felt cold. She opened her bag. The ticket wasn't there. She had left it on her desk. She got off, ran home, took the ticket and ran back. By that time it had started to rain, and she had forgotten her umbrella. When she finally arrived at the hall, it was half past seven. The concert had already started. The doors were closed. A guard told her that she had to wait for a break. Hanna sat down on the steps, wet and angry. She could hear the music through the wall. They had already played her favourite song. Then somebody sat down next to her. It was a girl of her age, also wet. "My train was late," she said. "I had waited for this concert for a year." They listened through the wall together and sang quietly. When the doors opened, they went in and saw the second half. It was fantastic. "I missed five songs," says Hanna, "and I found a friend. I think that it was a fair price."
