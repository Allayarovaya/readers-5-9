Lesson 9

The School in the Castle

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our English teacher gave us a topic: "The school of my dream". Most people wrote about a school without homework. I wrote this. My dream school is in an old castle on a hill. It is not dark or cold. The classrooms are spacious and full of light, with big windows and comfortable chairs. There are no desks in rows. We sit in a circle, so everybody can see each other. Lessons start at ten, because scientists say that teenagers think better later in the morning. There are only four lessons a day, but each one is long, and we really finish what we start. There is no school uniform. There are no marks either. Instead, teachers write short letters about what you did well and what you can do better. The teachers are tolerant. They don't judge anybody by appearance. If you have blue hair, nobody cares. If you ask a strange question, they say, "Good question. Let's find out together." In the afternoon there are extracurricular activities: a theatre in the big hall, astronomy in the tower and gardening near the old wall. You can choose any, or you can just read in the library, which has a fireplace. There is one strict rule. You must be kind. A student who laughs at others has to work in the kitchen for a week. My teacher wrote: "A wonderful school. But you have described many things that already depend on us, not on a castle." I read my essay again. She was right. Only the fireplace is missing.
