Lesson 7

You Must, You Can, You Should

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My older brother Yaroslav finished school three years ago. Before my first day in the eighth form he gave me his "survival guide". He wrote it on the back of an old test. "Listen carefully," he said. "Here is everything that I know. You must be on time for the first lesson. Teachers forgive many things, but they don't forgive being late on Monday. You mustn't look at your phone under the desk. You think that nobody can see you. Everybody can. Your face is blue. You have to do your homework on the day when you get it. I know that you won't. But I must tell you. You don't have to be the best at everything. Choose two or three subjects that you love and work hard at them. For the others, just do your work honestly. You should ask questions. When I was your age, I couldn't do it. I was afraid to look stupid. Then I understood that half of the class didn't understand either. You shouldn't laugh when somebody gives a wrong answer. Tomorrow it may be you. You can always talk to the school psychologist. It is not a shame. I did it twice. You may sometimes get a bad mark. It is not the end of the world. You must not hide it from Mum. She can always find out. And the last thing. You could be anything in this school: the clever one, the funny one, the sporty one. But you should be the kind one. People remember that." I have kept the old test. It is the best thing he has ever written.
