Reader Karpiuk 8 · Unit 6 · Different Choices, Different Lives
Lesson 2

Outside or Online?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our PE teacher did a survey in the eighth forms. He asked one question: "What did you do last Saturday?" The results surprised even him. Out of fifty-two students, thirty-one spent more than four hours on the internet. Nineteen didn't go outside at all. Only eight did any sport. He didn't shout. He put two lists on the wall and asked us to fill them in: "What the internet gives me" and "What activity gives me". The first list was long. The internet gives us friends who live far away. It gives us information for school, music, films and games. It teaches us things: one girl has learned to draw from videos, and one boy speaks good English because of online games. It is always there, even when it rains. The second list was shorter at first. Then it grew. Activity gives us energy. After an hour of football you are tired, but you feel alive. After an hour of scrolling you are tired, and you feel empty. Activity gives us real faces, real voices and real laughter. It gives us sleep: people who move during the day sleep better at night. And it gives us stories. Nobody says, "Do you remember that great evening when we all looked at our phones?" "I am not against the internet," said the teacher. "I use it myself. But your body was made to move. So here is a deal: one hour outside for every hour online." We tried it for a month. I didn't always manage. But on the days when I did, I fell asleep in five minutes.
