My Favourite Writer

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
My name is Lucas. I like reading books. My favourite writer is Daniel Defoe. He was an important English author. Many people consider him to be one of the founders of the English novel. During his lifetime, he published some 200 works of non-fiction and 2,000 short essays as well as his novels and other works of fiction. His books reflect his own interests and experiences. After he left university, he became involved in trade and politics, and traveled widely in Europe publishing his first poem in 1701. His most famous novel, Robinson Crusoe, was published in 1719, when he was 60 years old. This novel is about a young Englishman who dreams of going to sea. When his father refuses to let him make even one journey, he runs away to sea. A terrible shipwreck occurs and Crusoe ends up on a desert island. Crusoe has many adventures, but twenty-seven years after the shipwreck, he is finally rescued. I like this novel because it is rather interesting and full of adventures.
