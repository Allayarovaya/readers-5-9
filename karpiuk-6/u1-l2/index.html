Lucas: I’d like to know some facts about the English education. Ruby: What would you like to know? Lucas: Well, lots of things. To begin with, what subjects do you study? Ruby: We study English, History, Arithmetic, Elementary Mathematics, Science, Arts and Handicrafts. Lucas: Sorry, but I don’t quite understand what subject is which you called Science. Ruby: The subject called Science is really physics, chemistry and biology studied as one subject. Lucas: Do the pupils study foreign languages? Ruby: Yes, usually it is French or German. Lucas: Thank you very much.
