Peter: Hello, John! Come in. Mary: Hello, John! We are just going to have dinner. Will you have dinner with us? John: Thank you. I haven’t had dinner yet. Peter: Have a glass of juice. John: Thank you. Peter: Is dinner ready, Mary? Mary: Yes, it is. Help me to lay the table, please. Peter: With pleasure. Mary: Would you like to have roast beef and potatoes? John: Oh, it’s all right. I like roast beef and potatoes. Mary: And after dinner I’ll treat you to something tasty. Peter: What is it? Mary: We will have a delicious chocolate cake for tea. I have made it myself. Peter: Oh, great! We haven’t had a chocolate cake for a long time.
