My twin brother, Alex, is great at playing sports. He is fond of football and badminton. He goes to the sport club three times a week. We also play ball in the yard and on the sports ground. I am more interested in reading and music. I remember our birthday party. We had a lot of guests and got a lot of presents. We saw one huge present. Was it for both of us? Then Dad said, “Open it, Alex.” I was so jealous! My brother opened it quickly and inside was a bike. I hate riding bikes. I wanted a big exciting present, too. But Mum said that I got lots of other presents. I got many interesting books and a music box. I was happy, too.
