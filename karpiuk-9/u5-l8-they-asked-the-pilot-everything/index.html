Lesson 8

They Asked the Pilot Everything

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Last Thursday a real pilot came to our school. Captain Roman Hnatiuk has been flying passenger planes for nineteen years. Class 9-B had prepared questions, and the school newspaper wrote down the most interesting ones. The first question came from Bohdan. He asked the captain how long he had studied. The pilot answered that it had taken him five years to get his first licence and that he was still taking exams twice a year. Marta wanted to know if he had ever been afraid. Captain Hnatiuk said that fear was normal, but pilots were trained not to panic. He told us about a flight when a bird had hit the engine. He had landed the plane safely, and most passengers hadn't noticed anything. Yurii asked whether the job was well-paid. The captain laughed and said that it was, but that he paid for it with his time. He asked us how many family birthdays we had missed. Then he said that he had missed about forty. Sofiia asked what the best part of his job was. "The sunrise above the clouds," he said. "I have seen it a thousand times, and I still take photos." Then came the most popular question. Andrii asked if pilots slept during flights. The captain explained that on long flights there were always two or three pilots and that they rested in turns. He added that the autopilot didn't replace a human being. It only helped. Olia wondered why there were so few women among pilots. Captain Hnatiuk answered that the number was growing and that the best student in his flying school had been a girl. The last question was mine. I asked him whether he would choose the same profession again. He thought for a moment. He said that the job had two sides. On the one hand, there were long hours, nights in hotels and great responsibility for hundreds of lives. On the other hand, he had seen sixty countries and had never been bored for a single day. "Would I choose it again?" he repeated. "Ask me if a bird wants to fly." After the meeting four students asked the teacher where the nearest flying school was. One of them was Olia.
