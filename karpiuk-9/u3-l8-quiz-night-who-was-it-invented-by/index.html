Lesson 8

Quiz Night: Who Was It Invented By?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Every last Friday of the month the youth centre holds a quiz night. Teams of four sit at round tables, phones are collected in a box, and the quiz master, a student called Demian, reads the questions. Last Friday the topic was "Things Young People Love". Here are some of the questions. Can you answer them before you read the answers? Question 1. Where was basketball invented, and who was it invented by? The team "Sleepy Owls" wrote "the USA" and got half a point. The full answer: the game was invented in 1891 in the United States by James Naismith, a PE teacher who needed an indoor game for winter. The first baskets were real fruit baskets, and the ball had to be taken out by hand after every goal. Question 2. What were the first skateboards made of? Nobody knew. In the 1950s Californian surfers wanted to "surf" on the street when the sea was calm. The first boards were made of wooden boxes or planks with roller-skate wheels. Question 3. When was the first text message sent, and what was written in it? The team "No Idea" shouted "Hello!" and was wrong. The first SMS was sent in December 1992. Only two words were written in it: "Merry Christmas". Question 4. Is chewing gum a modern invention? No, it isn't. Tree resin was chewed by people thousands of years ago. Modern gum has been produced since the nineteenth century. Question 5. Which popular toy was designed by a teacher of architecture in 1974? Three teams got it right: the Rubik's Cube. It was created by the Hungarian teacher Erno Rubik as a model for his students. More than a month was needed before he could solve his own puzzle. Question 6. Where are the Olympic Games going to be held next? Every team answered correctly, and Demian said that the question had been too easy. At the end the points were counted. The quiz was won by "Sleepy Owls" with nineteen points out of twenty-four. The winners were given a cake, which was eaten by all the teams together. Has the topic of the next quiz been chosen? Yes, it has. It will be announced on the centre's page on Monday.
