Lesson 4

Ninety-Six Cakes and One Wheelchair Ramp

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our school is a hundred years old, and its front steps are very beautiful and very high. Nobody thought about them until September, when a new boy came to Year 7. His name is Ihor, and he uses a wheelchair. Every morning two teachers had to carry him up the steps. "It is not fair," said Liubov from our class. "He should be able to come in by himself." Liubov belongs to the school volunteer association, and when she says that something is not fair, things start moving. The volunteers went to the head teacher. She agreed that the school needed a ramp, but there was no money for it until next year. "How much does it cost?" asked Liubov. The answer was eighteen thousand hryvnias. "Then we will raise the money," she said. The plan was founded on three ideas. First, a charity fair. Every class had to bake or make something to sell. Second, a concert with tickets. Third, a sports challenge: teachers promised to pay ten hryvnias for every lap that students ran round the stadium. I was in the baking team. I had never baked anything except toast, but my grandmother was happy to teach me. On Friday evening our kitchen was covered with flour, and by midnight we had made ninety- six small honey cakes. Saturday was the busiest day in the history of our school. The yard was full of tables with cakes, knitted toys, wooden spoons from the wood crafting club and second-hand books. The school band played for two hours. And on the stadium two hundred students were running laps while the teachers counted and got paler and paler. Our PE teacher had to pay for 640 laps. He said it was the most expensive lesson of his life. In the evening the volunteers counted the money. We had raised twenty-three thousand hryvnias, five thousand more than we needed. The ramp was built in November. On the first day Ihor rode up alone, turned round at the door and waved to everybody in the yard. With the extra money the association bought a low desk for the library. Liubov is already working on the next project. I don't know what it is, but I have started learning to bake bread.
