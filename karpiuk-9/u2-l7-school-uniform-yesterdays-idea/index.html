School Uniform: Yesterday's Idea?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
The student council of our school organised a debate with a simple question: "Should we bring back the school uniform?" Two speakers from Year 9 were chosen. Here is what they said. Oleksandra, for the uniform. "Nowadays teenagers spend too much time and money on clothes. In my opinion, a uniform solves this problem. First of all, it saves time. You don't stand in front of the wardrobe every morning. Secondly, it makes everybody equal. Nobody can see whose parents are rich and whose are not. I believe that students should be judged by their ideas and not by the labels on their jeans. My grandmother wore a brown dress with a white collar to school. She says that it was not beautiful, but she never worried about what to put on. From my point of view, a modern, comfortable uniform would make school life calmer. All in all, a uniform is not a prison. It is a team shirt." Hlib, against the uniform. "I respect Oleksandra's opinion, but I disagree. I think that clothes are a part of our personality. At fifteen we are learning who we are, and our style helps us. From my point of view, a uniform teaches us to be the same, and the world needs people who are different. Besides, uniforms are not cheap. Parents have to buy them in addition to ordinary clothes. And do they really make us equal? Students will still compare phones, bags and trainers. In the past, schools were strict about everything: clothes, hair, even the colour of socks. I don't believe that those students learnt better than we do. To sum up, I think that we need a dress code, not a uniform. A few clear rules are enough: clean, neat, nothing offensive. The rest is our choice." After the speeches the audience asked questions, and then everybody voted. The result was close: 112 students voted for the uniform, and 131 voted against it. The head teacher thanked the speakers. "I think both of you are right in some way," she said. "Nowadays the most important thing is not what you wear to school. It is that you can disagree and still shake hands." Oleksandra and Hlib shook hands. She was wearing a white blouse. He was wearing a T-shirt with a dinosaur.
