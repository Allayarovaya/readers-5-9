Lesson 6

You Don't Have to Be a Genius

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
"I am not a genius, so I will never get a good job." Have you ever thought so? Then this article is for you. A large company once studied its best workers. The managers expected to find people with the highest marks and the most diplomas. They were surprised. Knowledge of a particular subject was important, but it was not the main thing. The best workers had several skills that are not written in any diploma. Skill 1. They can interact with people. Today almost nobody works alone. A doctor works with nurses, a programmer with designers, a builder with engineers. A person who can listen, explain and solve a conflict without shouting is worth more than a lonely genius. You train this skill every time you do a group project and don't kill each other. Skill 2. They learn fast. The world of work is changing quickly. Several professions that were popular twenty years ago have disappeared, and new ones are born every year. Nobody can learn everything at school. But you can learn how to learn: how to find information, check it and use it. Skill 3. They speak languages. The world is globalised. A farmer in Poltava sells sunflower oil to Spain. A designer in Lviv draws for a company in Canada. Your customer, your boss or your partner may live on another continent. English is no longer an advantage. It is a tool, like a hammer. Skill 4. They are not afraid of mistakes. In a particular experiment two groups made clay pots. The first group had to make one perfect pot. The second had to make as many pots as possible. At the end the best pots were made by the second group. They had made more mistakes, so they had learnt more. Skill 5. They finish things. Many people start. Few people finish. An employer will always choose somebody who completes a simple task on time over somebody who has a brilliant idea and forgets about it on Tuesday. None of these skills needs a special talent. Each of them can be trained, like a muscle. So the next time you think, "I am not a genius", remember: most good jobs are done by ordinary people with good habits.
