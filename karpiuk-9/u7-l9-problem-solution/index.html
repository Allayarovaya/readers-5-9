Reader Karpiuk 9 · Unit 7 · Think Green, Live Green
Lesson 9

Problem? Solution!

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
News about the environment is usually sad. But in every country there are people who don't wait for somebody else to act. Here are four problems and four solutions that started with one person or one small group. Problem: plastic in the sea. A Dutch teenager went diving on holiday and saw more plastic bags than fish. He was sixteen. Back at school he started a science project about cleaning the ocean, and a few years later he founded an organisation that builds floating barriers. They move with the wind and collect plastic from the surface of the water, and special boats catch rubbish in rivers before it reaches the sea. Problem: a city without trees. In a crowded Asian city there was no room for a new park. So the architects looked up. Now many buildings there have gardens on their roofs and walls. The plants cool the air in summer, clean it from dust and give a home to birds and butterflies. Problem: food that nobody eats. In many countries shops throw away fresh food at the end of the day. A group of students made an app. Cafes and bakeries offer what is left at a low price, and people nearby come and buy it in the evening. The shop loses nothing, the buyer saves money, and good bread doesn't go to a dump. Problem: a dead river. A river in a European town was so dirty that people called it "the grey snake". A retired teacher started cleaning one hundred metres of its bank near his house. Neighbours laughed, then helped. In ten years volunteers had cleaned the whole river inside the town, and the council built a modern filter station. Last spring fishermen caught the first trout there in half a century. What do these stories have in common? Nobody solved the whole problem at once. Each person took one piece that was close enough to touch. Our Biology teacher says that there are two kinds of people. The first kind says, "Somebody should do something." The second kind says, "I am somebody." The list of problems is long. Luckily, the list of solutions is still being written, and there is room for your name.
