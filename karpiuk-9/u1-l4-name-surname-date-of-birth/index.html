Lesson 4

Name, Surname, Date of Birth

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Zakhar wants to go to an international language camp in the Carpathians next summer. To get a place, he has to fill in an application form and have a short online interview in English. Today is the day of the interview. Coordinator: Good afternoon! I am Helen, the camp coordinator. What is your full name? Zakhar: Zakhar Bondarenko. Coordinator: Could you spell your surname, please? Zakhar: Of course. B-O-N-D-A-R-E-N-K-O. Coordinator: Thank you. How old are you, Zakhar? Zakhar: I am fourteen, but I will be fifteen in March. Coordinator: So what is your date of birth? Zakhar: The twelfth of March, 2012. Coordinator: And where are you from? Zakhar: I am from Ukraine. I live in Poltava. It is a city in the centre of the country. Coordinator: What is your address? Zakhar: 25 Sadova Street, flat 8. Coordinator: Now tell me a little about yourself. What are you keen on? Zakhar: I am keen on programming and cycling. I have also played the guitar for three years. Coordinator: Which languages do you speak? Zakhar: Ukrainian is my native language. I have learnt English since Year 1, and I started German two years ago. Coordinator: Why do you want to come to our camp? Zakhar: I want to speak English with people from different countries. At school we mostly read and write, and I am not confident when I speak. Coordinator: You are doing very well now! One last question. Is there any food you don't eat? Zakhar: Mushrooms. Coordinator: Nobody will make you eat them, I promise. You will get our answer by email next week. Zakhar: Thank you very much. Goodbye! A week later Zakhar opened his inbox. The first line of the letter said: "Welcome to the camp!"
