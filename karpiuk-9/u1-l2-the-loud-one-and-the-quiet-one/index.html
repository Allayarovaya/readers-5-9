Lesson 2

The Loud One and the Quiet One

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Everybody in our school knows the Melnyk twins. Ivanna and Solomiia have the same face, the same curly hair and the same birthday. But if you spend five minutes with them, you will never mix them up again. Ivanna is an extrovert. She gets her energy from people. She likes to be surrounded by friends, and the more of them there are, the happier she is. She is the head of the student council, she sings in the school band, and she knows the names of all the first-year pupils. When she is alone for more than an hour, she starts calling somebody. Solomiia is an introvert. It doesn't mean that she is shy or unfriendly. It means that she gets her energy from quiet time. After a noisy school day she needs an hour with a book or with her drawings. She has only two close friends, but she has known them since kindergarten, and she would do anything for them. For years people compared the sisters. "Why can't you be more active, like Ivanna?" teachers asked Solomiia. "Why can't you sit still, like your sister?" they asked Ivanna. Both girls hated it. Everything changed last year, when the class had to prepare a project about a role model. Ivanna wanted to speak about a famous singer. Solomiia chose a scientist. They argued for two days. Then Solomiia said, "Let's choose each other." So Ivanna told the class about her sister. "Solomiia is the most purposeful person I know," she said. "She spent a whole year learning to draw portraits, and she didn't give up. She has taught me to listen." Solomiia spoke about Ivanna. "My sister has the self-confidence that I am still learning," she said. "She is not afraid to make mistakes in front of people. Thanks to her, I joined the art club and showed my drawings for the first time." The class was silent, and then everybody clapped. Today the twins appreciate their differences. Ivanna has started a diary, because Solomiia says that it is good for self-development. Solomiia has agreed to sing one song at the school concert. "Only one," she says. "And then I will need a very quiet weekend."
