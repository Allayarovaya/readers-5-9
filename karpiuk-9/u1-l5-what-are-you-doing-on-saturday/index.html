Lesson 5

What Are You Doing on Saturday?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
It is Friday, the last lesson is over, and four friends are standing near the school gate. They want to spend Saturday together, but it is not as easy as it sounds. Darii: So, what are we doing tomorrow? Any ideas? Polina: I am not free in the morning. I am visiting my grandmother. We arranged it last week, and she is baking my favourite pie. Darii: What time are you coming back? Polina: The bus leaves her village at 12.40 and arrives in town at 1.15. Maksym: I am busy in the morning too. I am going to tidy my room at last. My mum says that she will not let me out until she can see the floor. I am going to start at eight, so I will probably be free by noon. Darii: OK, the afternoon then. Yeva, what about you? Yeva: I haven't decided yet. Look at those clouds. It is going to rain tomorrow, so the picnic by the river is not a good idea. Darii: Then let's go to the cinema. The new comedy starts at 3.30. Polina: I think it will be full. Everybody wants to see it. Darii: No problem. I will book the tickets online right now. Wait a second... There are four seats in the seventh row. Shall I take them? Maksym: Yes! I will pay you back tomorrow, I promise. Yeva: And I will bring popcorn from home. It is cheaper. Polina: Where are we meeting? Darii: Let's meet at three near the fountain. Don't be late. The film starts at 3.30, and they don't let people in after the lights go out. Maksym: I will be there at 2.55. You will see. Polina: Maksym, you are going to be late. You are always late. Maksym: Not this time. I am setting three alarm clocks. On Saturday it rained, just as Yeva had said. Polina's bus arrived on time. Darii came with the tickets, and Yeva brought a huge bag of popcorn. At 3.25 Maksym ran up to the fountain, out of breath. "The floor!" he shouted. "My mum saw the floor!"
