Lesson 6

Twenty-Four Hours Online

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our IT teacher gave class 9-V a strange homework. "For one day," she said, "write down everything you do on the internet. Every app, every search, every minute. Don't change your habits. Just count." On Monday we brought our notes, and four of us read them aloud. Severyn. I thought I was online for about two hours a day. My notes say five hours and ten minutes. I use a chat app from morning till night: I sent 214 messages on Saturday, and most of them were stickers. I watched Netflix for an hour and a half and posted two videos on TikTok. The only useful thing was a search on Google for my Geography project, and it took eight minutes. Uliana. I am online mostly for music. I download music for my dance classes and make playlists. I also follow three artists and one singer, and I read what they post. I don't post tweets or videos myself. I am a reader, not a writer. My total was two hours and forty minutes. I expected less. Nazarii. I do almost everything on the internet. I learn English with an app, I watch video lessons on Physics, and I search on Google twenty times a day. On Saturday I looked for a recipe for pancakes, the rules of table tennis and the height of Hoverla. I also played an online game for two hours. Total: six hours. My eyes hurt in the evening, and now I understand why. Myroslava. My day was different, because on Saturday I was hiking with my parents, and there was no signal in the mountains. I used a chat app for ten minutes in the morning and posted three photos in the evening. Total: twenty-five minutes. It was the best Saturday of the autumn. The teacher wrote our numbers on the board and asked two questions. "Which of these activities did you choose, and which just happened? Which of them will you remember in a month?" Nobody remembered the stickers. Nazarii remembered the pancakes, because he had really made them. Myroslava remembered the mountains. "The internet is a tool," said the teacher. "A hammer doesn't decide what to build." This Saturday Severyn is going hiking with Myroslava's family. He says it is an experiment.
