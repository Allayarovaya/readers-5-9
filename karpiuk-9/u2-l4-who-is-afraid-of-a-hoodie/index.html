Lesson 4

Who Is Afraid of a Hoodie?

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
A few years ago a large shopping centre in England made a strange decision. It decided to ban hooded tops. Youngsters who came in with their hoods up were asked to take them off or to leave. The managers said that customers were afraid of groups of teenagers in hoodies. The story was in all the newspapers, and people started arguing. Some agreed with the ban. Others asked, "What has got into these managers? A hoodie is a piece of clothing, not a crime." A group of students from a local school decided to see for themselves. They did a simple experiment. A fifteen-year-old boy called Jamie walked through the town centre twice. The first time he was wearing a hooded top, baggy jeans and trainers. The second time he had a school blazer and a tie on. Each time he stopped twenty people and politely asked the way to the library. The findings were surprising. When Jamie wore the blazer, eighteen people out of twenty stopped and helped him. When he wore the hoodie, only nine did. Several people crossed the street before he could open his mouth. One woman held her bag tighter. "I was the same person," Jamie said. "The same voice, the same question, the same smile. But in a hoodie I was sort of invisible and dangerous at the same time. It is not a nice feeling." The students also talked to teenagers with very different styles. A sixteen-year-old metalhead in a black leather jacket said that old ladies never sat next to him on the bus. "But I sing in a church choir on Sundays," he laughed. "You can't tell by looking at my boots." A girl with bright pink hair said, "People think I am rude. In fact I am shy. The hair speaks first, so I don't have to." The students presented their findings at a town meeting. "Clothes say something about us," they wrote, "but they don't say everything. When we judge a person by a hood, we learn nothing about the person. It shows only what we are afraid of." The shopping centre didn't cancel the ban at once. But a month later it invited the students to hang their project in the main hall. Most of them came to the opening in hoodies.
