Lesson 9

One Page About Me

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
In May the city zoo advertised summer jobs for teenagers: four assistants were needed for the children's farm. Kvitka, who is fifteen, decided to apply. The advert said: "Send your CV by 20 May." She had never written a CV in her life. Here is her first version. "Hi! My name is Kvitka, and I am a very good person. I love animals sooo much, especially rabbits. I am clever and beautiful, and I have many friends. I have no experience, but I learn fast. My hobbies are sleeping and TikTok. Please take me! P.S. I can't work on Mondays, Fridays and when it rains." She showed it to her aunt, who works in a personnel department. Her aunt read it and laughed for a minute. Then she took a pen. "A CV is not a letter to a friend," she said. "It is one page of facts. An employer spends about thirty seconds on it. Write only what is useful for this job, and put the most important things first." Together they made a new version. CURRICULUM VITAE Personal details. Kvitka Marchenko. Date of birth: 3 April 2011. Address: Chernivtsi. Phone and email. Education. 2017 to present: School No. 9, Chernivtsi. Year 9. Best subjects: Biology, English. Experience. 2024 to present: volunteer at the animal shelter "Four Paws" (feeding and walking dogs, cleaning cages, twice a month). Summer 2025: helped at my grandparents' farm (rabbits, hens, goats). Skills. English: good. Computer: text documents, presentations. First aid course for teenagers (certificate, 2025). Personal qualities. Responsible, patient with small children, not afraid of physical work. Interests. Books about animals, cycling, photography. References. Ms O. Bilyk, Biology teacher, School No. 9. "But I wrote almost the same things in my first version," said Kvitka. "No," said her aunt. "In the first one you said that you loved animals. In the second one you proved it." Kvitka sent the CV on 12 May. A week later she was invited to an interview. Out of thirty-one candidates, only six had sent a CV without spelling mistakes. She was one of them, and now she knows the name of every rabbit on the farm.
