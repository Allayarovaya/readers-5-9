Lesson 5

Four Friends, Four Styles

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our Art teacher asked us to make a photo project called "Style Is a Language". My three friends and I decided to photograph each other and to describe what we wear and why. We have been friends since Year 3, but nobody would ever say that we belong to one group. SportyVita. Vita is the captain of the volleyball team, and she dresses as if a match could start at any moment. She wears comfortable tracksuit bottoms, plain T-shirts and light trainers. Her clothes are never tight and never fancy. "I want to move freely," she says. Her only decoration is a bright orange cap, which she wears back to front. Classic Lev. Lev is the only boy in our school who irons his shirts. He likes neat, simple clothes in dark blue, grey and white: a smart checked shirt, straight trousers and clean leather shoes. He looks elegant even on a school trip to the forest. "Good clothes are like good manners," he says. "They show respect for other people." We laugh at him, but teachers adore him. Romantic Mariana. Mariana loves long flowery dresses, soft knitted cardigans and old-fashioned jewellery from her grandmother's box. Her style is gentle and a little dreamy. In winter she wears a huge woollen scarf that her aunt knitted for her. "I feel like a girl from an old novel," she says. She writes poems, of course. Street-style me. I am Arsen, and I wear what skaters wear: wide jeans, oversized T-shirts with bold prints and scruffy trainers that my mother wants to throw away. My favourite thing is a patterned jacket with eleven pockets. My style is messy, but every piece has a story. When we stood together for the last photo, the picture looked funny: a tracksuit, an ironed shirt, a flowery dress and a jacket with eleven pockets. "You look like four different shops," said the teacher. But that was the point of our project. Style is a language, and each of us speaks our own. We don't need to wear the same clothes to understand each other. We only need to listen.
