Lesson 6

Me in Ten Years

Listen & Read
Listen to the text
Speed:
Our class teacher gave us an unusual homework. "Write your profile," she said, "but not the one you have today. Write the profile of the person you are going to be in ten years. Then explain how you will get there." Here is what three of us wrote. Nazar, 15. In ten years I will be twenty-five. I am going to be a vet in a small town, and I will have my own clinic with a green door. I know it will not be easy. I am going to study Biology and Chemistry very seriously for the next two years. This summer I am working as a volunteer at an animal shelter. It is already arranged: I start on the first of June. I think I will be tired, but I will learn a lot. One thing is certain: in ten years I will still have a dog. Emiliia, 14. I am not sure about my future job, and I think that is normal. Maybe I will be a translator, maybe a designer. But I know what kind of person I am going to be. I am going to travel a lot, so I will need languages. Next month I am starting a Spanish course. The lessons begin on the third of October. I will also be braver. Today I am afraid to speak in front of the class, but I am going to join the debate club this term. I hope my hands will not shake so much in ten years. Yurii, 15. In ten years I will live in Kharkiv and build bridges. I have loved bridges since I was five. I am going to enter a technical university, and I will probably study for six years. My parents say that I will change my mind ten times. Perhaps they are right. But I am not going to wait. On Saturday I am taking part in a city competition for young engineers. My model is ready. It is a bridge made of pasta, and I hope it will hold four kilograms. When we read our profiles aloud, the teacher said, "Nobody knows what will happen in ten years. But a person with a plan will get further than a person with only a dream." Then she collected our papers and put them into an envelope. She is going to give them back to us at our school reunion.
